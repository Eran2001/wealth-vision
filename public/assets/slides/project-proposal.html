Project Proposal | September 2024

Wealth Vision

A system for investor profiling and personalized recommendations with ESG-aware global market guidance.

Research Problem

  • Investors face fragmented global markets and complex product choices.
  • Existing advisory tools often lack strong ESG integration.
  • Manual profiling can be inconsistent, slow, and difficult to scale.

Main Objective

Design and develop an intelligent platform that profiles investors and recommends suitable global investment options based on risk tolerance, goals, time horizon, and ESG preferences.

Proposed Components

  • Investor profiling engine
  • ESG scoring and integration module
  • Recommendation engine
  • Transparent dashboard for decision support
