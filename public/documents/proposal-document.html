Proposal Document

Project: Wealth Vision | Research Area: Investor profiling, ESG integration, and personalized recommendations

Research Problem

Global investing is complex because investors must compare many asset classes, regions, sustainability concerns, and risk profiles. Existing robo-advisory tools often lack meaningful ESG integration and global market coverage.

Main Objective

To design and develop Wealth Vision, an investor profiling and recommendation system that provides personalized, transparent, and globally inclusive investment options.

Specific Objectives

  • Capture investor risk appetite, goals, investment horizon, and ESG preferences.
  • Evaluate assets using ESG and market performance indicators.
  • Recommend suitable global investment options through predictive models.
  • Provide an explainable dashboard for reviewing recommendation logic.

Methodology

The system follows requirement analysis, system design, implementation, integration, and evaluation phases with quantitative and qualitative validation.
